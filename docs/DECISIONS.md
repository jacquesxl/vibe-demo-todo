# Decisions

Running log. Newest at the top. Filled from the session wrap-up prompt (two minutes at the end of each session), reviewed by you, never by hand from memory.

Kinds (closed list, exactly one per decision): business · product · design · technical · build · verify · operations.
If something does not fit, write "does not fit" and look at it again; it is usually two decisions tangled.

Rules: decisions only, never execution steps. A decision without a reason is a leftover suggestion. Anything the agent decided without you noticing gets logged here too, marked "unnoticed", and re-decided awake.

## YYYY-MM-DD

- **[kind] / [part of product]** — What was decided. Why (two or three sentences). Assumed: what has to stay true for this to hold.

## Parking lot

Good ideas that arrived at the wrong layer or the wrong time. Not rejected; waiting to be judged against alternatives when their layer comes up. Write the trigger that reopens each one.

- [idea] — arrived during [product/design conversation on ...]; revisit when [trigger].

## Deferred on purpose

- [decision] — deferred [date]; revisit when [trigger]. (A conscious deferral stops occupying you. An avoided one does not.)

## Only-you list

Decisions that need your judgment, not just your presence: what it is worth, who it is for, what you promise, what you refuse to build, where it goes next. Make these awake, on purpose, never in passing mid-build.
